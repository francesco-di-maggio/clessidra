Clessidra

Pour grains of sound from single drops into a dense cloud.

Hardware

Made in
Synthux Instrument Design Residency 2026
Licence
MIT

Clessidra is a granular instrument inspired by the hourglass. It explores the slow emptying of the glass and the fast trickle of single grains. It lets you capture and bend time through sound, slowing it down, speeding it up, reversing it or freezing it. Broken grains, like sand, are gathered and pieced back together, never quite the same and never fully still.
