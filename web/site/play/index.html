Sound · 8 sLED

Reading the Sound

Write point
Where the input is written. Orange while Fill is up, grey when sealed.
Selection
The shaded part: what the playhead, the line with a mark, moves through.
Input
The orange shade behind the write point: what grains following the input read. The dashed line moves through it.
Grains
One dot per grain, at the point it reads. Larger at the peak of its window. High: left, low: right.
Slices
P03–P09 under the sound, found when Fill seals it.

Playing

Knobs
Drag up and down. Shift for fine steps. Double-click: back to where it started.
Faders
Drag or click along the slot.
Pads
Hold to touch. Held longer, a touch becomes a firm press. With a pen, its pressure.
Keys
1–7 play the slices. Tab moves between controls, arrows turn them.
Input
Demo is a plucked phrase. Mic and files go into the input, as a cable into the board. Use headphones with the mic.
